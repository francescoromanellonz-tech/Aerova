brand

## Users

Two equally weighted audiences, served by parallel paths:

1. Affluent Vietnamese homeowners — health-conscious, lifestyle-driven,
   evaluating AEROVA against bottled delivery, in-line filters, and boiling.
   Mobile-first, bilingual (VN/EN), premium category buyer.
2. Vietnamese commercial buyers — facilities and procurement decision-makers
   for offices, hotels, restaurants, and cafés. Comparing AEROVA against
   bottled-water contracts and conventional commercial dispensers.

Both arrive with the same underlying job: trust this category enough to act.
Residential acts by ordering online; commercial acts by requesting a quote.

## Product Purpose

AEROVA sells the LT-AWG20G atmospheric water generator as the premium
category-defining solution for Vietnam: water from air, mineralized,
alkaline-balanced, seven-stage filtered. The site's job is to make two
distinct buyers believe in the category and the brand, then route each one
cleanly to the right action.

Success: the homepage gives every visitor a clear residential or commercial
path within one screen of arrival; the product page sustains belief through
specific engineering detail; conversion is split (order for homes, quote for
commercial) without forcing either audience through the other's funnel.

## Brand Personality

Considered. Atmospheric. Technical-luxe.

Quiet confidence backed by engineering specificity. Editorial pacing.
Cinematic when warranted, restrained otherwise. Voice is factual and
material: numbers, stages, materials, dB, TDS. Never hyped, never wellness-soft.
Aspirational without being cold.

## Anti-references

- Not appliance retail. No spec-sheet density, no price stickers, no
  rotating product-on-white shots, no electronics-store grid layouts.
- Not tech SaaS or dashboard. No gradient cards, no hero-metric clichés in
  colored circles, no repeating "icon plus heading plus text" feature grids.

## Design Principles

1. Two doors, one house. Every surface decides which buyer it serves first
   (residential or commercial) and offers each a clean primary action. No
   undifferentiated "Learn more" for everyone.
2. Show the engineering. Credibility comes from specific, technical detail
   (HEPA, 254nm UV-C, 75 to 150 TDS, 45 dB, 7 stages). Replace decoration
   with information that signals competence.
3. Quiet over loud. The product is designed to disappear into a serene space.
   Every visual choice mirrors that posture: editorial pacing, exponential
   ease-outs, restrained color, large breathing room.
4. Atmospheric, not aspirational. Air and water are the brand's materials.
   Lean on physics (mist, condensation, mineral, dew point) over generic
   luxury wellness imagery.
5. Vietnamese contemporary, not exotic. The site is bilingual and serves a
   local market. Cultural cues (ST25 rice, specialty coffee, Vietnamese
   interiors) belong as evidence of fit, not as ornament.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG 2.2 AA across both light and dark modes.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` (already implemented in CSS and GSAP).
- Bilingual VN/EN: preserve Vietnamese typography tracking and diacritic
  rendering; never strip diacritics for layout convenience.
- Mobile-first defaults; respect `Save-Data` for media-heavy surfaces
  (already honored by the canvas hero).
