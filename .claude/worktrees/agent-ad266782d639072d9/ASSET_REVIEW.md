## Machine Visual Identity Reference

Use this description in every prompt that includes the Aerova machine to ensure visual consistency across all generated assets.

**Form factor:** Floor-standing tower water dispenser. Slim, tall — approximately 30 cm wide × 130 cm tall. Narrower at the top, slightly wider base cabinet.

**Body finish:** Matte black throughout. Subtle fine-grain texture, not glossy. The top section (head unit) and lower cabinet are both matte black.

**Display panel:** Blue backlit LCD digital display on the upper-front face. Shows temperature and quality readings. Three small control buttons below the display.

**Dispensing area:** Two side-by-side lever taps at mid-height. Left tap has a red accent indicator (hot); right tap has a blue accent indicator (cold). Chrome/brushed-silver drip tray beneath both taps.

**Side ventilation:** Visible ventilation grille on the lower-right side panel.

**Filter access:** Front-loading filter compartment in the lower cabinet section.

**Style keywords:** matte black tower, premium, tech-forward, minimal seams, blue LCD glow, chrome accents, fits luxury Vietnamese interiors.

**Reference images on file:** `machine-diagonal view.png`, `machine-display view.png`, `machine-water dispenser view.png`, `machine-livingroom view.png`, `machine-lifestyle-modern-vietnamese-home.jpg`

### Filtration Stages (ProductPage — StickyPanel + Mobile Carousel)

**Stage 01 HEPA** — image: HEPA filter ✅  
**Stage 02 Condensation** — image: condensation coils ✅  
**Stage 03 Sediment** — image: PP sediment cartridge ✅  

**Stage 04 Pre-Carbon Filter** — image: MISSING  
> "Activated carbon absorption eliminates chlorine, volatile organic compounds, and unpleasant odours or tastes."  
Needs: close-up of a black activated carbon block, granular activated carbon (GAC) material, or carbon filter cross-section. Dark matte texture, teal/sage grade.

**Stage 05 Reverse Osmosis** — image: `aerova-uf-ultrafiltration-membrane-pure-water-filter.png` ⚠️  
> "RO membrane removes dissolved solids, heavy metals, bacteria, and contaminants at the molecular level — the gold standard of water purification."  
The filename says **UF (ultrafiltration)** — these are different technologies. UF uses hollow-fibre bundles; RO uses a spiral-wound membrane element (cylindrical white tube). The image should show a proper RO membrane element, not hollow fibres.

**Stage 06 UV-C Sterilization** — image: MISSING  
> "Ultraviolet light at 254nm destroys 99.99% of remaining bacteria, viruses, and pathogens without chemicals."  
Needs: glowing UV-C lamp (purple/violet tube), or water chamber with violet UV glow, dramatic and technical.

**Stage 07 PureSky Mineralisation** — image: `aerova-water-dispenser-tap-mineral-water-close-up.png` ⚠️  
> "Essential minerals — calcium, magnesium, and potassium — are restored to the purified water, bringing it to an optimal alkaline pH balance."  
Current image shows the dispensing tap — correct for the end result, but mineralisation as a _stage_ should ideally show the mineral media (ceramic balls, calcite stones, mineral crystals) or mineral-rich water. Usable for now; candidate for improvement.
