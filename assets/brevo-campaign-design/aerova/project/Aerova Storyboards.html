Aerova · Storyboards for AI image generation

Twelve frames,
drawn to brief.

Each frame below is a composition sketch, a written prompt, and a negative prompt. Paste the prompts into Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, or whichever model you prefer. The sketches are for you — they show what in-frame should look like, so you can reject generations that drift.

Aerova Water · Asia
Companion to the Asset Audit
12 frames · 3 priorities
Use with image gen

01 · Read the sketch first

The line drawing shows composition intent: where the machine sits, where the eye lands, what the negative space is doing. Don't use it as input to the AI — it's your mental model.

02 · Paste the prompt

Copy the written prompt into your generator. Keep the negative prompt if your tool supports it — it blocks the AI-slop tropes the audit calls out.

03 · Reject on drift

If the generation shows a generic dispenser, smiling families, cold fluorescent grade, or a faked "3/4 kitchen," it failed the brief. Regenerate or move on.
