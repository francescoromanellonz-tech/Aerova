What boiling actually removes.

Boiling still kills bacteria. That part has not changed.

But Vietnam's water in 2026 carries something boiling cannot touch.

Heavy metals. Arsenic. Lead. Manganese. They come from old pipes, industrial runoff, and contaminated groundwater. They are invisible, odourless, and tasteless. And they do not evaporate when you boil water. If anything, as the water evaporates the metals become more concentrated.
Boiling removes
Bacteria
Some pathogens
Boiling does not remove
Arsenic
Lead
Manganese
Industrial chemicals

What do these metals do to your body?

Lead builds up slowly. Over months and years, it affects concentration and memory. In children, even small amounts have been linked to learning difficulties. In adults, it raises blood pressure and puts strain on the kidneys.

Arsenic is connected to fatigue that does not go away no matter how much you sleep. Long-term exposure is linked to serious illness. Industrial chemicals like solvents affect the liver and hormone system.

None of these feel like water problems. They feel like getting older, or just being tired, or not sleeping well. That is what makes them so hard to trace.

The good news: the solution does not require complex chemistry. It requires starting before water ever reaches a pipe.

Quick question while this is fresh: when you boil your water at home, do you feel fully confident it is safe to drink? I am curious what most people actually believe.
