What pho is actually made of.

Most people know that what you put into a pot matters. The bones. The herbs. The time.

Fewer people know that the water itself changes the food.

Standard filtered water and most bottled water is put through reverse osmosis, which strips minerals entirely. When you cook with it, that mineral-hungry water pulls calcium and magnesium out of your ingredients during the boiling process.

The pho broth, the rice, the vegetables you cooked for your children — all less nutritious than they could be, because of the water underneath them.

Our water is mineralized after filtration. Calcium, magnesium, and potassium are restored before it reaches your tap. When you cook with it, those minerals stay in the food rather than being drawn into the water.

It is a small change underneath every meal your family eats.
