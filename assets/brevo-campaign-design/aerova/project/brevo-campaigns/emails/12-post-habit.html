Day 30 · One month in

A small stat, a habit forming.

A month ago you switched to water from the sky. By our estimate, your household has already kept about ninety plastic bottles out of circulation. In a year, that becomes around 1,100. Over the lifetime of the unit — closer to 11,000.

More to the point: you have probably stopped thinking about water at all. Which is exactly the point. The best infrastructure is invisible.
An invitation — only if it feels right

If you know someone who would benefit from this, we have a quiet referral programme.

They receive a Founders' Rate on their unit. You receive a credit toward filter replacements — or, if you prefer, a donation to a Vietnamese clean-water charity in your name.

No pressure to share. We would rather you keep the unit to yourself than recommend it to someone who is not the right fit.

Out of curiosity: in the last month, has anyone asked you about the unit? I am trying to understand how this category spreads — by tap, by glass, by conversation. Reply and let me know.
