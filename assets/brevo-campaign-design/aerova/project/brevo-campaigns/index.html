Copy & design principles applied across the system

  • Subject lines · 4 – 7 words, 20 – 40 chars. Visible on a mobile lock screen.
  • Body length · 75 – 150 words; education emails up to 220.
  • Paragraphs · Three sentences max. White space is the design.
  • One CTA per email · Multiple CTAs split attention.
  • Open question at the end · Every email invites a reply.
  • Sender name personal · "Franc from AEROVA" — never the brand alone.
  • No urgency, no hype · "Don't miss" and "Act now" trigger spam filters and erode trust in a premium category.
  • Cream over obsidian · The website lives in dark register. Email lives in the inbox — a softer, warmer expression of the brand.
Flow 01 — Welcome Sequence5 emails · Day 0 – 14

Triggered by Sky Water Guide download. Sequence is paced over 14 days, leading with category education and ending with a soft consultation invitation. Email 01 is plain text from franc@aerova.vn for inbox-placement reasons; Emails 02 – 05 use the HTML template.

Flow 02 — Nurture3 broadcasts · monthly cadence

Sent to the full subscribed list once a month after the welcome sequence completes. Pillars rotate: lifestyle (coffee), authority (global context), Vietnamese cultural anchor (cooking). Never sell — always teach or expand worldview.

Flow 03 — Re-engagement1 email · 30+ days inactive

Plain text. Lowercase subject. The absence of design is the design choice — looks like a personal note, not a marketing send.

Flow 04 — Post-purchase & Retention6 emails · Day 1 – Month 12

Triggered by purchase. The post-purchase experience is the pre-purchase experience for the next customer. Every touchpoint surfaces testimonial material, catches service issues early, and seeds referrals without asking. Anniversary email closes the loop and asks the advocate question.
