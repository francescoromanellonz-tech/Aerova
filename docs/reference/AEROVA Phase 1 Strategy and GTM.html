Section 01
Tone of Voice
Four principles that govern how we write. One reference table that governs what we never write. Consistency in tone is how a new brand builds trust before it has scale.
Characteristic 01
Confident Calm
We speak from certainty. We never explain ourselves defensively or oversell. A brand that has solved a real problem does not need to shout. Statements land, then breathe.
Characteristic 02
Precision Over Volume
Every sentence earns its place. The goal is one true thing said clearly, not several approximate things said enthusiastically. Specificity signals quality more than any adjective can.
Characteristic 03
Outcome Before Mechanism
"Pure, mineralized water on demand" before "7-stage filtration process." The mechanism is the proof. The outcome is the promise. Lead with what the customer experiences, not what the technology does.
Characteristic 04
Rhythmic Brevity
"No pipes. No plastic. No compromise." Three-beat structures create memorability without urgency. Short sentences. Full stops. Luxury brands control pace, and pace signals confidence.

Domain Warmup Protocol
Critical Before Any Email Send
A new email domain sent to bulk lists immediately lands in spam. Domain warmup is the process of gradually building sender reputation with mail servers. Follow this protocol before any marketing emails go out. Week 1: Send 10 to 20 plain-text personal emails per day from [email protected] to known contacts who will open and reply. No marketing content. Week 2: Send Email 01 (the founder plain-text) to the first 50 to 100 warm signups only. Week 3: Expand to 200 to 500 recipients per day using the HTML template. Week 4 onward: Scale to full list. Use a warmup tool (Instantly, Lemwarm, or Mailreach) to automate this in parallel. Monitor spam score with Mail-Tester.com before each send. Target score: 9 out of 10 or above.

Funnel Strategy
An omnichannel funnel where SEO, social media, email, and Zalo work together as a single connected system. Each lead enters through a different door. Each door triggers a different journey. Lead scoring determines when to push and when to wait.
The Governing Principle
No single channel converts on its own. TikTok creates curiosity. SEO captures intent. Email builds understanding. Zalo closes. The job of each channel is not to convert but to prepare the lead for the next channel. A customer who has watched a TikTok, read a blog post, downloaded the guide, and had a Zalo conversation converts at 3 to 5 times the rate of a cold contact. Build the ecosystem, not the individual touchpoints.
How the Channels Work Together
SEO: Captures Intent
People Searching for the Problem
Someone searches "is tap water safe in Ho Chi Minh City" or "plastic in bottled water Vietnam." Our blog post answers the question authoritatively and ends with the Sky Water Guide download. They land with high intent and no brand awareness. The email sequence educates them about the solution. Zalo closes. SEO leads are the warmest organic leads in the system because they were already looking.
Social Media: Creates Curiosity
People Who Were Not Looking Yet
A TikTok video stops someone mid-scroll. A Facebook carousel makes them think about their water spend for the first time. They are not searching for a solution but they are suddenly aware of a problem. Social media leads require more education before they are ready to talk. The email sequence does this work before Zalo is introduced. Never push Zalo to a cold social lead before Email 03.
Email: Builds Understanding
The Education Layer Between Channels
Email is the connective tissue. It receives leads from every other channel and moves them through a consistent education arc regardless of where they came from. By Email 04, every lead understands the technology, the health case, and the economics. Email also hands off to Zalo deliberately in Email 03 by introducing the OA as the place for questions, creating a natural permission-based channel bridge.
Zalo: Converts
Where Trust Becomes a Decision
Zalo is Vietnam's primary trust channel with 85% market share. Conversions via Zalo OA run 28% higher than traditional social media. Once a lead is in a Zalo conversation, the conversion dynamic shifts fundamentally in our favour. Zalo is not used for broadcasting to cold leads. It is introduced only after the email sequence has established context and permission. Every Zalo interaction ends with an open question and moves toward either a consultation booking or a direct purchase decision.
