## 1. AWG Explainer Paragraphs

These are the canonical sentences for explaining what AEROVA does. Use them verbatim or adapt slightly. Never deviate from the core claim: water from air.

### 25-word version (for Email 1 or SMS)
> AEROVA is a machine that produces up to 20 litres of pure, mineralised water per day from the air — no pipes, no bottles.

### 50-word version (for Email 2 opening, social captions)
> Vietnam's air runs at 75–85% humidity year-round. AEROVA draws that moisture in, condenses it, and runs it through 8 filtration stages — HEPA air scrubbing, reverse osmosis, mineral restoration, and dual UV sterilization. The result is alkaline drinking water at pH 7.4–8.2, produced continuously from the air above your property.

### 100-word version (for nurture emails, proposals)
> Most water problems in Vietnam share the same root: what comes out of the tap or gets delivered in a bottle cannot be trusted. AEROVA solves this differently. Rather than filtering compromised water or depending on a supply chain, the machine produces water from the air itself — drawing in humid Vietnamese air, condensing it to pure vapour, then running it through eight certified filtration stages including HEPA, reverse osmosis at 0.0001 μm, mineral restoration, and continuous dual UV sterilisation. No pipes. No deliveries. No single-use plastic. Up to 20 litres per day, always on.

### Vietnamese version (25-word equivalent)
> AEROVA tạo ra đến 20 lít nước uống tinh khiết mỗi ngày từ không khí — không cần ống nước, không cần bình nhựa.

## 4. ROI Calculation Formulas (Fill-in-the-Blank)

These are the calculation templates. Fill in the numbers during CRM research before writing the email.

### Villa Owner ROI Formula
```
INPUTS:
  Rooms/bedrooms:          [R]
  Estimated guests/night:  [G] (1.5 per bedroom is a safe default)
  Days occupied/month:     [D] (assume 65–75% occupancy for Airbnb Superhost)
  Current bottled water cost: [C] per month (estimate ₫15,000/person/day)

CALCULATION:
  Monthly water spend = G × D × 15,000 VND = [W] VND
  Annual water spend = W × 12 = [A] VND
  AEROVA investment = $5,500 USD = ~₫137,500,000 VND (at 25,000 VND/USD)
  Payback period = 137,500,000 ÷ (W per month) = [P] months
  
  Filter service: ₫1,200,000 per 6 months = ₫2,400,000/year

EMAIL LANGUAGE:
  "Based on [R] bedrooms and your occupancy rates, you're likely spending
   approximately ₫[W]/month on guest water. AEROVA pays itself back
   in [P] months — after that, the water is essentially free."
```

### Corporate Office ROI Formula
```
INPUTS:
  Employees:           [E]
  Current solution:    Water cooler / bottled / filtered (ask)
  Estimated cost/month: [C] (₫50,000–₫80,000 per person/month is standard for cooler + bottles)

CALCULATION:
  Monthly water spend = E × 65,000 VND average = [W] VND
  Annual = W × 12 = [A] VND
  AEROVA investment = $5,500 = ~₫137,500,000
  Payback = 137,500,000 ÷ W = [P] months

EMAIL LANGUAGE:
  "For [E] employees, your current water solution likely costs between
   ₫[LOW]M–₫[HIGH]M per month. AEROVA replaces this entirely.
   Payback in approximately [P] months."
```

### Hotel/Villa ROI Formula (Rooms-Based)
```
INPUTS:
  Rooms:           [R]
  Guests/room:     [G] (avg 2)
  Occupancy rate:  [O]% (use 60% default if unknown)
  Bottled water cost/guest/night: ₫30,000–₫60,000 (vary by tier)

CALCULATION:
  Monthly guests = R × 30 × O% × G = [MG]
  Monthly water cost = MG × ₫40,000 = [MW] VND
  Payback = 137,500,000 ÷ MW = [P] months
  Plastic bottles eliminated/month = MG × 3 bottles = [B] bottles

EMAIL LANGUAGE:
  "A [R]-room property at [O]% occupancy is spending approximately
   ₫[MW]/month on guest water. AEROVA eliminates that spend entirely.
   Payback: approximately [P] months. Bonus: [B] fewer plastic bottles
   per month — an increasingly important signal for eco-conscious guests."
```

## 5. Price Framing Blocks

These are the canonical sentences for introducing price. Never use "cost" — always "investment."

### The Daily Rate Anchor
> The investment is $5,500 — which, over a five-year service life, is $3.01 per day. Less than a bottle of mineral water from a hotel minibar.

### The Comparison Anchor (against bottled water)
> Bottled water at hotel rates: $0.50–$1.50 per 500ml. AEROVA: $3.01 per day for unlimited volume. The machine pays for itself in [X] months and produces water indefinitely after that.

### The Lease Option Framing
> For properties where a capital outlay isn't the right fit, AEROVA is available on a monthly investment basis — spreading the cost as an operating expense rather than a capital item. Speak to us about lease terms.

### Price with Payback (Email 5 template sentence)
> At $5,500, AEROVA pays for itself in approximately [P] months based on your current water spend — after which the water is produced at the cost of electricity alone (970W peak, typically $8–$15 USD/month in HCMC).

### The "Not a Discount" Reframe
> We don't discount AEROVA — the investment is the same for every customer. What we do offer is a pilot: a 30-day installation where you run the machine, measure the output, and decide with certainty rather than on a spec sheet.

## 7. Demo Invitation Language

The demo is the close mechanism in Vietnam. Getting a yes to a demo is the goal of Emails 1–4. Use these sentences.

### Standard invitation
> Would 15 minutes to see it working in person make sense? I can bring the machine to your property — there are no obligations and nothing to set up.

### With urgency (use only in Email 5+, not before)
> We have installation slots open in [Month] for the District 2 area. If you'd like to see the machine before committing to anything, I can arrange that this week.

### Post-reply (they asked a question, now invite them)
> Great question — it's actually much clearer to see in person than to explain in an email. Are you available for 15 minutes this week or next? I'll bring the machine.

### Zalo version (shorter, conversational)
> Xin chào [Name], đây là Francesco từ AEROVA. Bạn có muốn xem máy hoạt động trực tiếp không? Tôi có thể mang máy đến chỗ bạn — chỉ cần 15 phút.
> *(Hello [Name], this is Francesco from AEROVA. Would you like to see the machine working in person? I can bring it to you — just 15 minutes.)*

## 8. Pilot Offer Language

The pilot offer is the conversion mechanism — removes all risk and fits Vietnamese decision-making culture.

### Standard pilot offer
> Rather than asking you to decide on a specification, we'd prefer you to decide on experience. We offer a 30-day pilot: the machine is installed at your property, you run it at full capacity for a month, and you decide with certainty. If AEROVA isn't right for you, we collect it — no cost, no obligation.

## 9. Objection Handler Scripts

### "It's too expensive"
> The investment is $5,500 — and at your current water spend of approximately $[X]/month, that pays back in [P] months. After that, the water costs roughly what your electricity bill already does. The question isn't whether it's expensive — it's whether spending $[X]/month indefinitely on delivered bottles makes more sense than a one-time investment that produces water from the air above your property.

### "We already have a water solution"
> Most of our customers said the same thing before they replaced it. What you have now — whether bottled delivery, a filter system, or a cooler — has an ongoing cost and a logistics dependency. AEROVA eliminates both. What would it be worth to never order water again?

### "We need to think about it"
> Of course — this isn't a decision to make in a day. What would be most helpful: a site visit so you can see the machine working at a comparable property, or a detailed proposal with figures specific to your operation?

### "The GM doesn't make this decision — I need to check with the owner"
> That's exactly how it should work. Can I prepare a one-page summary you could forward to the owner? It covers the payback period specific to your property, the plastic elimination figures, and the 30-day pilot option. Most owners find the numbers straightforward once they see them.
> *(Note: this response gives them a tool to sell upward — Vietnamese GMs often need this)*

### "What about maintenance? Who fixes it if it breaks?"
> AEROVA comes with a 2-year parts and labour warranty, and we provide free installation in HCMC and Hanoi. Filter cartridges are serviced every 6–18 months; the LCD display alerts you by cartridge number when each is due. For a property your size, this typically means one service visit per year at ₫1.2M–₫2.4M.

### "I've never heard of this type of machine"
> Very few people in Vietnam have — it's a new category here. In Australia and Singapore, atmospheric water generators have been in commercial use for over a decade; Vietnam's humidity makes it ideal. That's also why we offer the 30-day pilot: we'd rather you form your opinion from experience than from a brochure.

## 11. Subject Line Formulas

These formulas generate subject lines. Fill in the variables for each prospect.

### Formula 1: The Observation
`[Specific thing I noticed about their property] — one question`

Examples:
- `KN Holiday Villa — a question about guest water`
- `Your Airbnb listing in An Phu — one question`
- `[Hotel Name] — a question about your water amenity`

### Formula 2: The Local Social Proof
`How [similar local property] eliminated [cost/problem] at [property name]`

Examples:
- `How a boutique hotel in Hội An replaced their water delivery entirely`
- `How a 7-bedroom villa in District 2 eliminated 3,500 bottles per month`

### Formula 3: The Number
`[Specific number] — [what it means for them]`

Examples:
- `3,500 fewer plastic bottles per month`
- `$3.01/day for unlimited pure water at [Property Name]`
- `20 litres of alkaline water, made from the air above your property`

### Formula 4: The Question
`What do your guests drink when they're thirsty at [Property Name]?`

### Formula 5: The Follow-Up
`Re: AEROVA — something concrete this time`
`Following up — the 8 stages between air and a glass of water`

### Formula 6: Price Email (Email 5 only)
`The investment figure for [Property Name] — and your payback period`

## 12. Brevo Personalisation Tokens

When building templates in Brevo, use these contact attribute tokens:

| Token | Attribute | Example output |
|---|---|---|
| `{{contact.FIRSTNAME}}` | First name | Francesco |
| `{{contact.LASTNAME}}` | Last name | Romanello |
| `{{contact.COMPANY}}` | Company/property | KN Holiday Villa |
| `{{contact.CITY}}` | City | Ho Chi Minh City |
| `{{contact.UNITS_ROOMS}}` | Rooms/units | 7 |
| `{{contact.SCORE_LEVEL}}` | Lead score | Hot |
| `{{contact.LEAD_TYPE}}` | Segment | villa |
| `{{params.payback_months}}` | Calculated payback | 14 |
| `{{params.monthly_spend}}` | Current water spend | ₫4,200,000 |
| `{{params.demo_date}}` | Scheduled demo | 20 May 2026 |
| `{{params.pilot_start}}` | Pilot start date | 1 June 2026 |

**How to personalise at scale in Brevo:**
- Static attributes (name, company, city, rooms) — set when contact is created/imported
- Dynamic params (payback, demo date) — pass via transactional API call or set before sending a campaign
- For segmented campaigns, use Brevo's "List" or "Segment" feature to send the right email to the right people without conditional blocks in every template
